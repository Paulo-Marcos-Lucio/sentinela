# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors
and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, offensive language, personal attacks, and
any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to **contatopml26@gmail.com**. All
complaints will be reviewed and will result in a response deemed necessary
and appropriate to the circumstances.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
